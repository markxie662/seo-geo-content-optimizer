# Your voice memo transcripts, arranged

Hush is a local desktop app for individuals who want to label and search their own voice memo transcripts. It runs offline on macOS 14 or later.

Import text transcripts you already have, add labels, and search your transcripts when you want to find something again.

## Bring your transcripts

Hush works with text transcripts of your voice memos. Have your transcripts ready before importing them: Hush does not record audio or transcribe recordings.

## Built for personal, offline use

Use Hush to organize your own transcripts on your Mac. Sync and collaboration are not supported.

Before buying, check that your Mac runs macOS 14 or later and that you have the text transcripts you want to import.
